主标题：cliff church
副标题：Anchored within Bonifacio's eroded cliffs, architectural corridors interconnect three native reefs to frame a newly unified scenic vision.
正文：Bonifacio is a city on the southern tip of the French island of Corsica, a tourist destination and a port terminal. the ancient city of Bonifacio is a different kind of landscape, standing on a high and narrow white promontory, which has been eroded by water and wind, creating a unique karst style. Because it is an ancient city, there are 17th and 18th century buildings as far as the eye can see and the white cliffs are like a fortified wall, adding a mysterious, ancient and unbreakable feeling .

There are about 6 large and small churches in the ancient city, and there is also a monastery in the easternmost part of the city, and there are also religious services on festivals which can be said to be full of religious atmosphere. The new town of Bonifacio, separated from the old city by a bay, is a place of commercial and tourist development, with only more modern entertainment activities.
In order to better connect the two areas on the same island, I analyzed and explored the surrounding environment and chose a good location to connect the old and the new town. On the one hand, it complements the lack of religious architecture in the new city, and on the other hand, it echoes the old city. The basic functions of the traditional church elements in the old city are extracted and new functional attributes are formed. The project site has three coral reefs of different sizes but closely connected, which are linked together by architectural corridors to form a new vision. The new church mainly provides convenience to the residents of the new city, but also brings a new landscape to the old Bonifisio.
